## Scoring

our-score = impact × confidence ÷ effort (each 1–5), same scale as `docs/BACKLOG.md`.
"Lossless" = byte-identical greedy output expected; "numerics" = deterministic but last-bit rounding may change
(must be checked with the output hash and treated as lossy if it differs); "lossy" = changes routing.

## Checked and not applicable / already covered

- **Qwen3.6 INT4 CUDA series** (#2983–#2998: tiled expert matmul +13 %, long-prefix split attention +50 % prefill on L4,
  fused SwiGLU/RoPE), **#3138** (whole-step CUDA graphs, Qwen3.8 125B 120 → 197 tok/s), **#3155**, **#3107/#3121**
  (CUDA arena, GPU-side misses): CUDA-only. The ideas (tile by expert, split long-prefix attention) already exist in
  ggml-metal's `mul_mat_id` and FA split-K.
- **#2850** (concurrent miss reads, prefill lookahead `PF_AHEAD`, PILOT pool; Xeon: TTFT 83.7 → 53.5 s): superseded on
  our side by io-threads and layer-major (fetch 6 s of ~154 s). The backlog item "whole-next-layer prefetch during
  prefill" (5.3) is moot.
- **Qwen3.6 GGUF on Metal** (#2307): their GGUF path is 2× slower in prefill than their own format largely because a GGUF
  expert is three reads; our PGRN sidecar already stores one record per expert.
- **Read-ahead via `F_RDADVISE`** (#2936, +14 % on Qwen3.8 Metal): we do real lookahead prefetch already.
- **#3048** (route KV slots by exact prefix hit + LRU): llama-server has similarity-based slot choice and LRU; the
  app runs one slot.
- **#3094** (stripe reads over two SSD replicas): single internal NVMe.
- **Prompt rows 2048 vs 1024** (+4 % at 8192 tokens): we measured ubatch already (−20 % for 4096 without layer-major,
  neutral with it).

## Papers (2026), quick scan

- [arXiv 2611.15842](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15842) — Fewer Expert Transfers Without Changing Routing (NeurIPS'26
  workshop): Belady eviction copies 2.1× fewer experts than LRU; 16 tokens of foresight recover 96 % of it; causal
  decayed-frequency recovers ~27 % (12–15 % fewer transfers); routers trained for cache hits did not reduce real
  transfers; holding expert sets fixed always cost ≥ 0.067 nats. Supports item #7 and is a caution for routing-side
  ideas.
- [arXiv 2611.14848](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14848) — Tideway (Duke): phase-aware tiered memory for MoE agents on
  consumer PCs (prefill layout vs decode layout, CUDA graphs under paging); TTFT 2.40×, TPOT 1.61× vs best baseline.
  CUDA/PC-centric; the phase-aware idea is what layer-major + item #5 give us.
- [arXiv 2610.36564](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36564) — DeepInfer-Mac: 750B-class DeepSeek V4.1 on a 24 GB MacBook Air,
  338 GiB on the internal SSD. Relevant to the Laguna "bigger than RAM" pitch, not to Qwen3.6 now.
- Colibrì cites arXiv 2609.04575 for the quality cost of dropping experts on Qwen3.6-35B-A3B (6 of 8 experts:
  −1.10 MMLU points; 4 of 8: −3.10). Useful as a reference point for Turbo's quality text.

## Where this contradicts or updates `docs/BACKLOG.md`

1. **Cross-conversation prefix reuse (4.0, effort 5)** — likely overestimated effort: upstream llama-server already
   creates checkpoints at user-message starts and keeps them with `--cache-ram` states. Measure first (item #1,
   effort 1); only the disk part (item #4) and block sharing (item #14) are real work.
2. **Prefill progress in the cockpit (10.0)** — with layer-major (`-b 40960`) the prompt is one `llama_decode`, so the
   server's per-batch progress no longer moves. Needs a per-layer hook, which also gives cancellation (item #3).
3. **Stale rows:** "Cold insertion of prefill reads (7.5)" and "Prefill cache pollution measurement E3 (6.0)" are
   still open, but E3 was measured and rejected (neutral) on 2026-11-26. "Layer-(group)-major prefill (5.0)",
   "Prefill baseline + profile (doing)" and "Cheap prefill levers (doing)" are done per RESULTS. "Whole-next-layer
   prefetch during prefill (5.3)" is moot after layer-major.
4. **Lookahead top-6 (4.0)** — Colibrì's own gain is 0 % on prose and +3–5 % on code/chat with a 512 MiB cache; at our
   4–10 GiB expect less. Keep, but as a cheap A/B, not a sure win.
5. **MTP single draft ≥ 0.6 (3.0)** — Colibrì's MTP results on M1 are mixed (−6.5 % … +10.9 %), and our app disables MTP
   for streamed runs for a measured reason. Lower confidence; only the combined "MTP-lite" package (item #17) has a
   chance.
